# NCCF-2024-BATCH-AI
Interactive Power BI dashboard using real life data, analyzing the demographics, educational backgrounds, state of origin, house distribution, and accommodation preferences of NCCF Ekiti Batch A1 NYSC members, providing insights to support member profiling and accommodation planning.

## 📊 Key Insights

### 1. Survey Overview
A total of **60 Batch A1 corps members** residing in the NCCF Ekiti Family House participated in the survey.

### 2. State of Origin Analysis
- Corps members represent **19 states** across Nigeria.
- **Delta State** recorded the highest representation with **11 members**.
- **Abia State** and **Rivers State** followed with **5 members each**.

### 3. Regional Distribution
- **South-South:** 28 members
- **South-East:** 16 members
- **North-Central:** 8 members
- **South-West:** 7 members

The South-South region recorded the highest representation among respondents.

### 4. Gender Analysis
- **Female:** 32 members
- **Male:** 28 members
- **Confirmed permanent residents:** 14 members, comprising 8 females and 6 males.

### 5. Academic Specialization
- **Computer Science:** 7 members
- **Accounting:** 4 members
- **Economics:** 4 members
- **Microbiology:** 4 members

Computer Science was the most represented academic discipline among the reported specialties.

### 6. Residence Intention Analysis
- **Confirmed to stay:** 14 members (23.3%)
- **Undecided:** 17 members (28.3%)
- **Confirmed to leave:** 29 members (48.3%)

Nearly half of the respondents indicated their intention to leave the NCCF Family House, highlighting the importance of understanding members' accommodation preferences for effective residence planning.
